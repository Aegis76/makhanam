Makhana (Euryale ferox) is not merely cultivated; it is nurtured in Bihar's ancient, mineral-rich floodplains. For over nine months, lotus blossoms float upon pristine waters, absorbing trace minerals and sun energy before sinking into nutrient-dense sediment.

The 7-Stage Lotus Journey

From waterbed silt to your private collection, each seed undergoes seven patient transformations masterminded by lineage artisans.

STAGE 01 / 07

Pond Wetland Cultivation

In early November, organic seeds are sown in natural wetlands (maans). The seeds sink into rich organic silt where they germinate beneath calm, nutrient-packed aquatic layers.

"The wetland sediment provides natural iron and magnesium that no controlled greenhouse can ever replicate." — Master Harvest Artisan, Darbhanga
